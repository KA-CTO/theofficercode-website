For students who join The Officer Code Mentorship Program

I hereby confirm my enrollment in The Officer Code Mentorship Program under Mr. Anand Mohan and agree to the following:

    Program Contribution: I commit to honour the program contribution schedule as discussed and agreed upon at the time of joining, without delay.
