Before you ask

Quick questions, honest answers.

Is this really 1-on-1 with Anand Sir?

Yes. Your sessions are with Anand Mohan Sir himself. Not a junior mentor, not a recorded video.

What happens in a session?

You and Anand Sir go through your week: what you studied, where you got stuck, your weak topics, your test scores, and the plan for the next week.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I take this?

Yes. The plan starts from where you are. If you're new to UPSC, your first few sessions build the base before anything else.

How is the Weekly plan different from the Bi-Weekly one?

Weekly includes answer writing evaluation — every week you submit answers and Anand Sir marks them like an examiner. It also meets twice as often, so weak spots don't build up.
