About The Officer Code

You don't have to go to Delhi to prepare for UPSC. At TOC, you can prepare right here in Ranchi, or from your home.

Who We Are

A UPSC Coaching Centre in Ranchi

The Officer Code, or TOC, helps students prepare for UPSC. We also guide students for BPSC and UPPCS.

Our classroom is in Ranchi. We also teach live online, so you can join from any city.

Classes are taken by Anand Mohan Sir. He has been through UPSC Prelims, Mains and Interview himself. So he knows the exam from the inside.

TOC was started by Mr. Anand Mohan and Mr. Saman Shrestha.
